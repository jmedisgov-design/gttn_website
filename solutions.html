Buyers and procurement teams

Pay for what you verified, not for what you were told.

  • Start a deal with a description, the seller and a price; add details later.
  • Find the seller by name and country; no registration number needed.
  • See what GTTN is verifying for this kind of goods, ticked as each is resolved.
  • Pay in stages: each instalment is held until its condition is met.
  • Buy from a company or a private seller; buy as a company or as yourself.

Suppliers

Be the seller buyers can trust from the first message.

  • Verify your company once and appear in the directory of verified suppliers.
  • Offer goods with their evidence already checked.
  • Follow every deal a buyer starts with you and send your documents to it.
  • Get paid on clear, agreed conditions instead of open-ended disputes.

Banks and lenders

Finance assets you have evidence for.

  • Assess an asset and its owner for financing, or for an asset-based loan or lease.
  • See each condition a lender relies on, its evidence and who completes it.
  • Know whether the same collateral was offered to another GTTN lender.
  • Keep a record that stands up in credit review and audit.
Talk to us →

Insurers

Cover what exists, where it is, owned by whom.

  • Assess an asset for cover: existence, location, ownership and condition.
  • Independent inspection and imagery where the goods allow.
  • One evidence trail from underwriting to claim.
Talk to us →

Trade and export finance

Finance real trade, once.

  • Check the supplier, the buyer (debtor), the invoice and the documents behind it.
  • Check shipment and the goods, and the supplier's bank account.
  • See whether the same invoice was presented to another GTTN financier.
  • Support for development-finance and export-credit requests.
Talk to us →

Private buyers, at home or from abroad

Buy across borders without taking it on trust.

  • Sign up as yourself with your name and country of residence.
  • Land, a house, a building project, a vehicle, machinery or goods.
  • Independent inspectors confirm progress before each payment.
  • A relative or agent near the asset can help, without deciding or paying.
