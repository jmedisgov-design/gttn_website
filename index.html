The problem

Everyone checks the same deal separately, and nobody checks it all

In high-value trade the buyer relies on the seller's word, the bank and the insurer repeat their own due diligence, payment details can be changed by a fraudster, the same invoice can be financed twice, and the goods themselves are rarely verified before payment.

1

Who is the seller?

A registered company, a private person, or someone using another's name.

2

Do the goods exist?

Do they belong to the seller, are they free of charges, are they what the documents say?

3

Do the papers agree?

Invoice, contract, certificates and shipping documents, and the account the money goes to.

4

When is it safe to pay?

Paying in full upfront leaves no protection; paying late breaks the deal.

What GTTN does

One evidence record for the whole deal

GTTN assembles, verifies and preserves the evidence from authoritative registers, tax authorities, satellite imagery, independent inspectors and the parties themselves, and shows everyone in the deal what is confirmed and what is still open.

Counterparty

Company registers, identifiers and tax authorities, or for a private seller, identity checked by GTTN staff.

Goods and assets

Registers and lien searches where they exist, satellite and location checks, and independent inspection.

Documents and accounts

Consistency across invoices, contracts and certificates; the payee account checked against the verified seller.

Payment in stages

Each instalment is released only when its condition is met. GTTN does not hold your money.

How we work

Evidence, not opinions

Official sources first

Registers and authorities come before commercial data, used only within their terms. Nothing is scraped.

Never a false "verified"

A correct format is not a confirmation, and an unavailable source is never held against anyone.

Every check shows its source

Each result records where it came from and when, so a bank, an auditor or a court can follow it.

You keep your money

GTTN decides when a payment may go; your bank or an authorised payment partner moves it.
