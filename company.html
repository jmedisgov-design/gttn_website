Company

Infrastructure for trust between strangers in trade

GTTN (Global Trusted Transaction Network) is headquartered in the United Arab Emirates and built for global use.

Why GTTN exists

Cross-border trade runs on trust between parties who rarely meet. The information that would justify that trust is spread across registers, tax authorities, inspectors, banks and documents, and each party in a deal assembles its own partial picture. GTTN joins that evidence on the deal itself, so every party can see what is confirmed before money moves.

Where we are

GTTN is a pre-seed company. The platform runs end to end in a test environment. We are preparing it for production, raising our first round of funding and inviting design partners whose transactions will shape the product. We say plainly what exists and what does not: we do not yet have customers, partnerships or licences, and we will announce them only when they are real.
