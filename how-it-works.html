How it works

From first contact to a completed, recorded deal

Verify who you are dealing with. Verify what you are buying. Then pay.

  1. DISCOVER
  2. VERIFY
  3. TRUST
  4. CONTRACT
  5. PAY
  6. DELIVER
  7. CONFIRM
  8. RECORD

1. Start with what you know

Describe what you are buying, who is selling and the price. GTTN finds the seller in the registers, chooses the checks for that kind of goods and starts them. Identifiers, location and documents can follow.

2. GTTN checks, behind the scenes

Company and tax records, the payee account, registers and liens, satellite imagery and location, document consistency. What needs a person, such as an independent inspection or a review, is assigned with a due date.

3. Pay in stages, on evidence

Each instalment has a condition: a signed contract, a passed inspection, confirmed delivery. When the evidence is in, the payment can go, from your own bank or through an authorised payment partner.

4. Keep the record

The deal closes with a complete evidence record: who checked what, from which source and when, shared with the parties who need it.

Trust and security

Designed for institutions that answer for every decision

Sources you can name

Every result records its source, time and method. Licensed data is used only within its licence.

Privacy by design

Tax and account identifiers are encrypted and shown masked; personal data is kept only as long as needed.

Clear roles

Who may start a deal, approve a payment or review evidence is set per organisation. No one reviews their own evidence.

No custody of funds

GTTN decides when a payment is eligible; banks and authorised payment partners move the money.

Explainable checks

Rule-based, evidence-cited analysis. Optional language-model assistance only with your organisation's consent.

Global from the start

Any country, any goods, many languages, including Arabic.
